For many years, I worked in the automotive industry, developing hands-on skills in repair, fabrication, and creative problem-solving. In 2025, I decided to turn my passion for building into something more personal by starting B4 Lighting. Today, I create one-of-a-kind custom lamps using a combination of vintage, reclaimed, and modern parts, giving each piece its own unique character and style. When I'm not in the workshop, I enjoy spending time with my wife, our dog Buddy—who's usually right by my side while I work—our kids Molly and Justin, and our newest addition to the family, our grandbaby Autumn.
